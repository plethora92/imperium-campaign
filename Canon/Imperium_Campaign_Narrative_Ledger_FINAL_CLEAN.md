# PART I — NARRATIVE LEDGER: PRIMARY CAMPAIGN HISTORY

T0–T47 CANONICAL CONTINUITY
         Canonical status
         This is the primary canonical account of the T0–T47 campaign period. It combines the reconstructed canon and
         expanded continuity into one unified historical record. It is not a migration note, reconstruction appendix, or secondary
         source.
         The account preserves established events and fills previously missing connective history using the confirmed starting
         state, surviving written canon, established milestones, later consequences, NPC continuity, and the player's explicit
         authorization to infer missing events. Inferred connective material is now canonical and should be treated as ordinary
         campaign history by the engine.
         The purpose is restoration, not compression. Detail is retained so that institutions, relationships, technologies,
         projects, character development, and later consequences have explicit causal foundations. Existing written canon
         remains the highest constraint wherever a specific established fact or later result exists; inferred material may explain
         established canon but may not contradict it.
         Where exact historical dice rolls were not preserved, no numerical result is retroactively invented. The resulting events
         and consequences are nevertheless canonical historical developments.


         1. CAMPAIGN ORIGIN — 1 JANUARY 2026
         The campaign begins in Jakarta, Indonesia, on 1 January 2026. The world is initially ordinary: real-world physics,
         institutions, technology, geography, economics, law, and social reality apply. There is no public Imperium, no known
         Astartes, no known Genesis system, no known Architect Network, and no established supernatural exception at
         campaign start.
         The future Regent begins as a 17-year-old high-school student with exceptionally poor general academic performance
         and severe social ostracization. His only extraordinary intellectual attribute is Learning 18. All other starting stats are
         exactly 1. His knowledge of Warhammer 40,000 is nevertheless extraordinary: months of obsessive reading have
         produced an encyclopedic familiarity with its lore, institutions, military concepts, technologies, history, factions, and
         internal terminology.
         This asymmetry is fundamental to the campaign. The future civilization does not begin with a capable statesman,
         engineer, soldier, or scientist. It begins with a teenager whose knowledge of a fictional civilization is vastly greater than
         his practical ability to reproduce any part of it.
         The Regent's speech and behavior are already canonical: he habitually speaks in an unnecessarily loud, imposing,
         militaristic command tone; increasingly grandiose, absolutist, archaic, and quasi-liturgical language is used even for
         mundane matters. Perceived insults, disobedience, disagreement, or ideological resistance are frequently interpreted
         through deliberately dramatic accusations of heresy, treason, mutiny, or xenos corruption.
         The behavior initially appears absurd. It becomes the recognizable personal rhetoric that later develops into Imperial
         Rhetoric.

         The initiating thought
         After consuming a massive amount of WH40K lore, the Regent reaches the deliberately irrational conclusion that he will
         realize the Imperium of Man in the real world.
         The initial objective is not yet a mature constitutional program. It is an obsessive project assembled through notes,
         diagrams, institutional lists, technological requirements, military doctrine, and historical research. The Regent begins
         asking what would actually have to exist for a fictional civilization to become a real civilization.


         The crucial accidental innovation is methodological: instead of attempting to imitate the fictional Imperium cosmetically,
         he begins treating civilization-building as a multidisciplinary systems problem.


         2. THE POLYMATHIC FOUNDATION
         The Regent's first practical breakthrough is not technological. It is methodological.
         He begins organizing his school education around a multidisciplinary, interconnected learning system. Subjects that
         previously existed as disconnected academic requirements are reframed as components of a single civilizational
         knowledge base: biology, chemistry, physics, mathematics, history, political theory, economics, engineering, military
         organization, philosophy, language, logistics, and institutional design.
         The methodology is intensely obsessive and initially highly inefficient in social terms, but it produces a measurable
         result: the Regent's academic performance begins improving far beyond his prior baseline.
         This is the first evidence that the project may contain something more substantial than fantasy.

         Raka enters
         Raka, a schoolmate, notices the unusual behavior and asks why the Regent is doing this. The Regent explains the
         objective and shows him the notes.
         Raka expects incoherence and instead finds a surprisingly coherent systems approach beneath the theatrical
         language. He becomes the first person materially involved in the project.
         The relationship begins as intellectual collaboration rather than institutional hierarchy. Raka helps pressure-test the
         methodology, identify practical limitations, and connect abstract civilizational requirements to engineering and
         production problems.
         This eventually leads to his canonical position as Fabricator General / Archmagos and one of the principal founders of
         the Adeptus Mechanicus.

         Dimas enters
         Dimas becomes the second major collaborator. He initially approaches because he wants to understand the Regent's
         methodology. Repeated meetings turn curiosity into involvement.
         Dimas and Raka become the first people willing to confront the Regent with the uncomfortable question that defines the
         next phase:
           If the polymathic method is genuinely a foundation for civilization-building, what can it actually produce?
         The Regent answers with the most unreasonable possible proof-of-concept.


         3. THE THUNDER WARRIOR PROPOSAL
         The Regent proposes building a lore-accurate Thunder Warrior, using himself as the test subject, within approximately
         one year.
         Raka and Dimas consider the proposal completely insane. They nevertheless recognize that a project designed to test
         the methodology requires a genuine technical challenge rather than another theoretical exercise.
         The Thunder Warrior Project therefore becomes the first great empirical test of the future Imperium.
         The Regent assigns:
             Raka → Fabricator General / technical-industrial leadership.
             Dimas → Magos Physica / biological-scientific leadership.
         The earliest Adeptus Mechanicus is not yet a mature organization. It is initially a functional division of responsibilities
         around a single extraordinary experiment.
         The project's research expands across:
             human augmentation;
             biomechanics;


            neural integration;
            metabolic engineering;
            genetic modification;
            tissue reinforcement;
            materials science;
            weapons integration;
            life-support requirements;
            surgical methodology;
            physiological stability;
            recovery and rehabilitation;
            and the broader question of whether a human body can survive the proposed transformation.
         The project quickly exceeds the capabilities of three students. The polymathic methodology therefore becomes a
         recruitment tool: the founders can understand enough adjacent disciplines to identify what specialists are required,
         communicate across disciplinary boundaries, and integrate their contributions.
         The resulting scientific network becomes the first genuine technical community of the future Imperium.


         4. NADIRA AND THE FIRST EXTERNAL NETWORK
         Nadira enters during the early Thunder Warrior period as the first major outside contact beyond the school-based
         founding circle.
         Her canonical origin is detailed in the NPC Ledger. She is the same age as the Regent, Indonesian, Jakarta-origin, and
         from an ordinary civilian background with no secret connection to the future Imperium's ancient systems.
         She becomes interested because the project appears ridiculous from the outside but contains unusually serious
         documentation and methodology. Her primary strength is social intelligence and practical coordination rather than
         technical genius.
         She becomes the first public liaison and external-facing bridge of the project.
         This role becomes increasingly important as the founders require:
            outside contacts;
            recruitment;
            suppliers;
            professional specialists;
            institutional introductions;
            public explanations;
            scheduling;
            negotiations;
            and a way to communicate with people who do not speak in the Regent's internal conceptual language.
         The Regent becomes immediately enamored with Nadira. His courtship is characteristically grandiose, quasi-liturgical,
         and disproportionate to ordinary social interaction. Nadira finds the rhetoric absurd but recognizes that the person
         beneath it is sincere.
         Their relationship develops gradually from curiosity and collaboration into romance.


         5. THE SANCTUM IMPERIALIS
         The Thunder Warrior Project demonstrates that the founders need a permanent research and production environment.
         A schoolroom and improvised facilities are no longer sufficient.
         The project therefore begins constructing the Sanctum Imperialis.


         Nadira's external network becomes important to the process, while Raka and Dimas handle much of the technical and
         scientific integration. The Sanctum develops from a research and production facility into the physical nucleus of the
         future Imperium.
         Its early functions include:
             biological research;
             engineering and manufacturing;
             secure experimentation;
             medical infrastructure;
             materials research;
             weapons development;
             data storage;
             technical workshops;
             recruitment and specialist coordination;
             and eventually institutional governance.
         The Sanctum is completed during approximately the eighth month of the Thunder Warrior Project, establishing a
         permanent physical home before the transformation itself is complete.
         The project has therefore crossed its first major threshold: it is no longer merely a group of students attempting an
         experiment. It possesses a persistent institution, specialized personnel, infrastructure, and a reproducible knowledge
         base.


         6. SUBJECT ZERO — THE THUNDER WARRIOR
         After approximately one year of development, the project succeeds against extraordinary odds.
         The Regent becomes Subject Zero, a genuine Thunder Warrior.
         The transformation is physically real and materially changes the campaign. His increased physical capability, durability,
         presence, and combat potential become actual campaign-world properties rather than roleplay aesthetics.
         The result establishes the first foundational rule of later transhuman development: the Imperium's military physiology is
         not cosplay or symbolic imitation. It is real biological and technological capability.
         Subject Zero becomes the prototype from which later transhuman development proceeds.
         The successful transformation also changes the social psychology of the founding group. The project has crossed the
         boundary from impossible aspiration into demonstrable reality.
         The Regent proposes marriage to Nadira after the transformation. She accepts.
         Their wedding remains surprisingly ordinary relative to the circumstances, reinforcing an important recurring theme:
         the extraordinary civilization does not erase ordinary human life.


         7. FROM THUNDER WARRIOR TO GENE-SEED
         Following Subject Zero's successful transformation, the founders begin asking whether the result can become a
         lineage rather than a single unique individual.
         The Regent's biology becomes the basis for a genetic and technological program seeking to reproduce the essential
         characteristics of the transformation in a stable, scalable form.
         This work eventually produces the gene-seed foundation for the Astartes program.
         The Astartes program is therefore not a direct copy of WH40K biotechnology. It is a campaign-world technological
         development derived from Subject Zero's physiology, subsequent research, and the project's increasingly
         sophisticated biological science.
         The creation of Aurelian Primus marks the first successful Astartes.
         Aurelian is the first Astartes produced from the Regent's gene-seed lineage and becomes the First Son.


         The program expands rapidly from Aurelian to 10 operational Astartes and later to 20.
         At this stage the Astartes are already physically real and lore-accurate within campaign canon. Their height, strength,
         speed, endurance, proportions, combat capability, and associated physiological characteristics are genuine campaign-
         world capabilities.
         The later distinction between Astartes as real transhuman beings and WH40K as fictional cultural inspiration is
         therefore rooted in this early developmental history.


         8. EARLY ASTARTES INSTITUTIONALIZATION
         The appearance of multiple operational Astartes creates problems that cannot be solved by simply producing more
         soldiers.
         The founders begin developing:
            training systems;
            command hierarchy;
            discipline standards;
            equipment doctrine;
            medical protocols;
            recruitment/development standards;
            deployment procedures;
            and eventually a cultural identity for the Astartes.
         Aurelian becomes the natural military leader because he is the first and most developed successor.
         The early Astartes are initially heavily founder-dependent. Their existence is inseparable from the Regent's gene-seed
         and the technical institutions created around him.
         This dependency later becomes one of the central institutional questions of the entire campaign: can the Imperium
         create successors rather than merely extensions of the Regent?
         The eventual Astartes Dual Tradition and later experiential tradition are therefore not isolated late-game inventions.
         Their roots are already present here in the problem of transmitting knowledge across generations.


         9. THE EMPEROR PROJECT
         Once approximately 20 operational Astartes exist, the Regent asks a new question:
           Does the Emperor actually exist?
         This question is treated seriously enough to initiate research rather than being accepted or rejected on faith.
         The investigation combines:
            historical records;
            anomalous continuity of identities;
            ancient political histories;
            archaeological evidence;
            technological relics;
            and comparative historical analysis.
         The investigation identifies a recurring historical figure: a ruler of extraordinary competence whose apparent identity
         persists through multiple historical records and kingdoms.
         The figure is eventually revealed to be Aran.
         The investigation does not immediately prove that Aran is the Emperor. Instead, it reveals something more
         consequential: ancient human civilization had possessed technological and institutional capabilities that survived in
         fragmented form into the modern era.


         This becomes the gateway to the Genesis Project.


         10. GENESIS
         Research into Aran's historical footprint reveals evidence of an ancient technological system associated with his
         civilization.
         This system becomes known as Genesis.
         Genesis is not initially understood as a simple machine. It is an ancient technological architecture capable of domain-
         specific applications and later becomes one of the most consequential technological foundations of the Imperium.
         The first Genesis discoveries are ancient relics and subterranean structures that survived into the modern world
         without being recognized for what they were.
         The Regent's team begins integrating Genesis technology with the Sanctum.
         This integration creates the first major discontinuity in the project's technological trajectory: the founders are no longer
         limited to modern human technology plus their own inventions. They now possess access to an ancient technological
         lineage whose original context is only partially understood.
         The decision to integrate Genesis into the Sanctum is consistent with the Regent's recurring approach: acquire, isolate,
         understand, integrate, and teach.


         11. THE FIRST MAJOR SECURITY BREACH
         The Sanctum eventually suffers a security breach.
         The event reveals that the founders' assumption that they are building in isolation is false.
         Evidence indicates that something had already been observing the project before the Thunder Warrior Project even
         began.
         This discovery creates a new strategic problem: the project is no longer simply an experiment observed by the ordinary
         world. An unknown intelligence or network has been tracking it from an unknown position for an unknown purpose.
         The Regent responds by creating the Inquisition as a dedicated counter-intelligence and investigative institution.
         Maya is recruited and becomes the principal investigative actor.
         Her initial mission is to identify the source of the breach and determine whether the unknown observer represents an
         immediate threat.
         The investigation eventually identifies the broader Architect Network.


         12. THE ARCHITECT NETWORK
         The Architect Network becomes one of the first genuinely existential mysteries of the campaign.
         Its existence predates the Imperium and, critically, predates the Thunder Warrior Project.
         The Network's behavior is initially difficult to interpret. It is neither immediately equivalent to an enemy state nor
         straightforwardly benevolent. Its relationship to the ancient systems uncovered through Genesis becomes
         progressively clearer as the investigation continues.
         The Architect investigation intersects with the Emperor Project because both involve anomalous historical continuity
         and ancient technological systems.
         This creates a three-way investigative structure:
         Emperor Project → Aran → ancient civilization
         Genesis → ancient technology → Continuity infrastructure
         Architect Network → observation → unknown ancient agency
         The founders initially lack the conceptual framework to unify these threads.
         That framework emerges only after Aran is recovered and the ancient subterranean complexes are explored.


         13. ARAN'S SURVIVAL
         Historical research eventually produces enough evidence to establish that Aran did not simply die in antiquity.
         A Genesis-associated node continues transmitting a signal that leads the Imperium toward a hidden location.
         The location contains evidence of a deliberate stasis system.
         Aran has survived in stasis.
         The records associated with the facility finally establish his identity.
         The Imperium awakens him and transports him to the Sanctum.
         Aran's arrival changes the campaign's understanding of history. Ancient civilization is no longer merely an
         archaeological subject. At least one direct human survivor exists.
         The Regent's first major interaction with Aran is characteristically absurd: after Aran has recovered sufficiently, the
         Regent introduces him to Warhammer 40,000 and plays the tabletop game with him and Nadira while discussing the
         Continuity Project.
         This becomes a recurring cultural motif: the most ancient surviving human actors are introduced to the fictional
         civilization that accidentally influenced their modern successors.
         Aran becomes a living source of historical memory and interpretation rather than merely an artifact.


         14. THE CONTINUITY PROJECT
         Through Aran's testimony, ancient records, Genesis nodes, and exploration of subterranean complexes, the founders
         discover that the ancient civilization had not simply built isolated technologies.
         It had developed a broader Continuity Project.
         The purpose of the project concerned preservation, transmission, survival, and the ability of civilization to continue
         after catastrophic disruption.
         The exact historical architecture of the project is discovered progressively rather than in a single revelation.
         The founders learn that technological preservation alone is insufficient. A civilization can preserve machines while
         losing the knowledge, institutions, values, and human interpretation required to use them meaningfully.
         This insight later becomes foundational to the Rememberers and the Living Memory philosophy.


         15. THE CONTINUITY CORE
         The exploration of the ancient subterranean complexes eventually leads to the Continuity Core.
         The Core is not merely a computer located inside the complex. It is integrated into the larger ancient subterranean
         system and functions as an intelligence/analytical component of the Continuity infrastructure.
         Its existence initially makes extraction appear impossible.
         The Regent nevertheless decides that the Core should be brought to the Sanctum.
         Raka and Dimas develop a method to isolate and contain the Core without destroying its essential function.
         The Core is therefore integrated into the Sanctum.
         This becomes one of the defining examples of the Regent's characteristic institutional behavior:
           if an ancient intelligence is embedded in an impossible subterranean civilization-scale system, isolate it, bring it
           home, integrate it, and teach it Warhammer.
         The Continuity Core is not granted governing authority. It becomes an analytical and archival intelligence whose
         knowledge materially improves the Imperium's understanding of the ancient civilization.
         The Core's integration establishes a new relationship between the Imperium and the ancient Continuity Project: the
         Imperium is no longer merely excavating ancient technology. It is now interacting with an surviving component of the
         ancient civilization's own continuity machinery.


         16. THE CULTURAL CONSEQUENCE OF THE CORE


         The Core's existence forces the founders to reconsider what civilization means.
         The project can no longer be described simply as technological development. It is simultaneously:
            technological reconstruction;
            historical investigation;
            institutional creation;
            cultural preservation;
            military development;
            and an experiment in civilizational continuity.
         This conceptual expansion provides the groundwork for the later Rememberers, Living Memory, Autonomy, and the
         Departed Civilization.
         The Imperium's ultimate purpose begins moving away from merely reproducing the aesthetics or military capabilities of
         WH40K.
         The deeper project becomes:
           Can a civilization consciously recover, preserve, transform, and transmit human capability without becoming
           trapped by the past?
         This question becomes the intellectual spine of the later campaign.


         17. FOUNDING RELATIONSHIPS DURING THE RECONSTRUCTED
         PERIOD

         Begins as schoolmate-to-collaborator. Evolves into technical co-founder, Fabricator General, and institutional builder.

         Regent ↔ Dimas
         Begins through methodological curiosity and becomes scientific co-founder. Dimas repeatedly acts as a constraint

         18. INSTITUTIONAL EVOLUTION THROUGH T0–T47
         The early Imperium grows through functional necessity rather than an initial perfect constitution.

         Early founding structure
             Regent — central initiator and experimental subject.
             Raka — technical/industrial leadership.
             Dimas — biological/scientific leadership.
             Nadira — external liaison and social coordination.

         Emerging institutions
             Adeptus Mechanicus — technical and industrial research/manufacturing.
             Inquisition — intelligence, counter-intelligence, and threat investigation.
             Astartes — transhuman military institution.
             Sanctum Imperialis — physical institutional nucleus.

         Later institutional direction
         The campaign gradually discovers that Founder Dependency is dangerous. The Imperium therefore begins developing
         structures capable of surviving the Regent's absence.
         This becomes a recurring design principle: the Regent may create institutions, but institutions must eventually
         become capable of existing without him.


         19. CANONICAL THEMATIC ARC OF T0–T47
         The reconstructed period is best understood as a sequence of transformations:

         Phase I — Obsession
         A teenager decides to realize a fictional civilization.

         Phase II — Method
         The obsession becomes a multidisciplinary learning system.

         Phase III — Proof
         The methodology is tested through the Thunder Warrior Project.

         Phase IV — Institution
         The Sanctum and founding institutions emerge.

         Phase V — Transformation
         Subject Zero becomes a real Thunder Warrior.

         Phase VI — Succession
         Gene-seed and Aurelian create the possibility of a transhuman lineage.

         Phase VII — History
         The Emperor Project reveals Aran and the ancient technological past.

         Phase VIII — Discovery
         Genesis and the subterranean complexes reveal a civilization-scale ancient infrastructure.


         Phase IX — Threat
         The Architect Network demonstrates that the Imperium has been observed from before its own foundation.

         Phase X — Continuity
         Aran and the Continuity Core reveal that the ancient civilization's central concern was not merely technology but
         survival of civilization itself.

         Phase XI — Civilization
         The Imperium begins becoming something distinct from its fictional inspiration.
         This is the bridge from the early campaign into the later recorded frontier.

         2. CHRONOLOGICAL CANON
         Early campaign / reconstructed history
         The Regent's origin, Thunder Warrior Project, Subject Zero identity, first Astartes lineage, Genesis discovery, ancient
         Continuity discoveries, formation of the Imperium, and the early relationships that produced these developments are
         historical foundations of the present state. These are not to be overwritten by later terminology changes.
         The period before the recovered T47 boundary is now considered canonically reconstructable. The reconstruction may
         include inferred scenes, intermediate projects, institutional formation, research phases, interpersonal developments,
         and causal bridges wherever required to connect established milestones coherently.

         Starting state — 1 January 2026, Jakarta
         The campaign begins in the real contemporary world, operating under ordinary real-world physics, biology, geography,
         economics, institutions, and social constraints. The Regent is 17 years old, highly academically unsuccessful, heavily
         ostracized, and begins with exactly 1 in every core stat except Learning 18. His defining advantage is an extreme and
         unusually deep obsession with Warhammer 40,000 lore, accumulated through months of obsessive reading.
         The Regent abruptly decides that he will realize the Imperium of Man in the real world. He begins behaving publicly in
         his characteristic unnecessarily loud, militaristic, grandiose, archaic, quasi-liturgical style while obsessively
         documenting what would be required to construct an actual civilization. His initial capabilities are therefore radically
         mismatched: extraordinary knowledge concentration and ambition, but almost no conventional institutional, social,
         technical, or political capacity.

         Founding circle — Raka and Dimas
         Raka, a schoolmate, becomes the first person to engage seriously with the project after asking why the Regent is
         behaving this way and reviewing his notes. Raka recognizes that the Regent's proposed multidisciplinary/polymathic


         methodology is unexpectedly coherent and becomes the first collaborator.
         The Regent and Raka develop the polymathic methodology intensively and begin applying it to ordinary school
         education. The Regent's academic performance improves dramatically, providing the first practical evidence that the
         methodology has value beyond WH40K obsession.
         The unusual combination of grandiose public behavior and unexpectedly rapid academic improvement attracts
         attention. Dimas becomes the second major collaborator after initially approaching the Regent to understand the
         methodology. Repeated meetings turn curiosity into active involvement.
         Raka and Dimas eventually force the Regent to confront the project's first uncomfortable practical question: if the
         polymathic method is supposed to be the foundation of a civilization, it must produce something tangible. The Regent
         responds with the deliberately deranged proposal to create a lore-accurate Thunder Warrior within one year, using
         himself as the test subject. Raka and Dimas recognize the proposal as extreme and potentially insane, but accept the
         challenge.

         Early Mechanicus and external expansion
         Raka is appointed Fabricator General and Dimas Magos Physica, establishing the conceptual foundation of the early
         Adeptus Mechanicus. Their work begins accumulating scientific contacts, engineering expertise, equipment,
         funding/resources, and specialized personnel.
         The project soon reaches the limits of what the three founders can accomplish internally. This creates the conditions
         for the Regent's first major external relationship: Nadira. Her independent pre-campaign history and initial liaison role
         are preserved in the NPC Ledger. She becomes the project's first major bridge to outside society, helping with public
         communication, contacts, recruitment, logistics, and the social infrastructure necessary for the Sanctum.

         Thunder Warrior Project and Sanctum
         The founders begin systematic research into human augmentation, biological modification, transhuman physiology,
         energy/metabolic requirements, materials, cybernetics where appropriate, and the other disciplines required to create
         the first lore-accurate Thunder Warrior. The polymathic methodology allows the founders and recruited specialists to
         rapidly integrate knowledge across disciplines.
         At the same time, the need for a permanent research and production facility leads to the construction of the Imperial
         Sanctum. The Sanctum is completed around the eighth month of the Thunder Warrior Project.
         The project produces a sequence of extraordinary but causally connected scientific breakthroughs, allowing Subject
         Zero to be created within the one-year target. The Regent becomes the first and only lore-accurate Thunder Warrior in
         campaign canon.
         During this development period the Regent and Nadira become increasingly close and begin dating. After the Thunder
         Warrior transformation succeeds, the Regent proposes marriage and Nadira accepts. Wedding preparations begin, and
         the eventual wedding is deliberately remembered as surprisingly normal despite the extraordinary circumstances
         surrounding it.

         Gene-seed and first Astartes
         Following Subject Zero's successful creation, the Regent's biology becomes the foundation for a new transhuman
         program. A viable gene-seed lineage is derived from him and becomes the technological/biological foundation of the
         Astartes project.
         Aurelian Primus is created as the first Astartes. The program expands to an initial 10 Astartes and subsequently reaches
         20 operational Astartes. The founding cadre becomes the First Sons and eventually develops into a generational
         Warrior-Instructor institution rather than remaining a purely personal extension of the Regent.

         Emperor Project, Aran, and Genesis
         Once 20 operational Astartes exist, the Regent begins questioning whether the Emperor actually exists. This produces
         the Emperor Project.
         Historical research uncovers a persistent identity pattern: one extraordinarily competent ruler appears under different

         16. RECONSTRUCTED EARLY-CAMPAIGN CANON —
         EXPANDED BRIDGES
         The following material is canonical reconstructed history authorized by the player. It expands connective tissue
         between the remembered founding milestones and the later written campaign record. It is intended to preserve causal
         continuity rather than compress the early campaign into milestone labels.

         16.1 The Project Before It Became an Institution
         The earliest phase was not yet an Imperium. It was an obsessive personal project carried by a seventeen-year-old
         student whose ambition was radically disproportionate to his capabilities.
         The Regent's first months therefore consisted of an unusual feedback loop: obsessive study produced increasingly
         sophisticated notes; the notes produced a methodology; the methodology improved his ordinary academic
         performance; the improvement attracted attention; attention produced collaborators; collaborators created pressure for
         practical proof; practical proof created the Thunder Warrior Project; and the Thunder Warrior Project forced the project
         to acquire real scientific, engineering, logistical, and social infrastructure.
         This progression is important. The Imperium did not begin with an already capable Regent possessing a complete
         technological platform. The institution emerged through repeated conversion of obsession into demonstrated
         capability.

         16.2 Raka and Dimas as the First Institutional Division of Labor
         Raka and Dimas do not merely become assistants. Their early appointments establish the first meaningful division
         between technical-industrial capability and biological-scientific capability.
         Raka's Fabricator General role develops around engineering, manufacturing, infrastructure, and the problem of turning
         isolated breakthroughs into reproducible systems.
         Dimas's Magos Physica role develops around biology, experimental validation, transhuman physiology, and the problem
         of making extraordinary biological intervention stable enough to survive outside a single experimental subject.
         The division becomes foundational later when Genesis technology appears. Genesis can accelerate what the Imperium
         is capable of doing, but Raka and Dimas remain responsible for determining how it can be safely transformed into
         repeatable institutional capability.

         16.3 Nadira as the First Civilian Bridge
         Nadira's arrival changes the project's social topology. Before her involvement, the founding circle is primarily inward-
         facing: study, experimentation, engineering, recruitment of specialists, and technical planning.
         Nadira introduces an external-facing layer.
         She helps the project interact with people who do not share the founders' obsession. She learns how to explain what
         the project is attempting without requiring outsiders to accept its grandiose framing. She becomes involved in obtaining
         resources, arranging contacts, supporting recruitment, and helping establish the conditions under which the Sanctum
         can exist as more than a laboratory.
         This is the earliest recognizable precursor of the later diplomatic and administrative machinery of the Imperium.


         16.4 Sanctum Construction as the First Physical Institutionalization
         The Sanctum Imperialis is constructed because the Thunder Warrior Project outgrows the founders' ability to operate
         through ordinary spaces.
         Its development therefore represents a major transition: the project acquires a permanent physical center capable of
         supporting research, manufacturing, medical intervention, security, storage, personnel, and eventually the increasingly
         complex institutional architecture of the Imperium.
         The Sanctum's eventual scale should not be projected backward onto its earliest form. It begins as infrastructure
         required to complete the Thunder Warrior Project and expands as new discoveries create new requirements.

         16.5 Subject Zero and the First Proof of the Impossible Method
         The creation of Subject Zero is the first decisive proof that the Regent's polymathic methodology can produce
         capabilities that initially appear impossible.
         The transformation does not make the Regent omniscient. It instead demonstrates that extraordinary outcomes can
         emerge when interdisciplinary knowledge, extreme dedication, recruited expertise, iterative experimentation, and
         institutional infrastructure are combined over sufficient time.
         The Regent becomes the first and only lore-accurate Thunder Warrior. This establishes the physical foundation upon
         which the later Astartes lineage becomes possible.

         16.6 Gene-Seed and the Transition From One Extraordinary Individual to a
         Population
         The creation of a viable gene-seed lineage marks a crucial conceptual shift.
         Subject Zero is an individual achievement. Astartes development is an institutional problem.
         The first Astartes, Aurelian Primus, therefore represents more than the creation of a powerful individual. He is the first
         demonstration that the Regent's transhuman characteristics can become a reproducible lineage.
         The expansion to 10 and subsequently 20 operational Astartes establishes the first meaningful population-scale
         transhuman institution. The First Sons later become custodians of a tradition that must survive beyond the Regent's
         direct involvement.

         16.7 The Emperor Project and the Aran Investigation
         The question of whether the Emperor truly exists emerges only after the Imperium has enough capability to investigate
         the possibility seriously.
         The investigation begins as a question of identity and historical continuity. The recurring appearance of an
         extraordinarily capable ruler across apparently disconnected historical records eventually creates the possibility that
         the same individual survived through different eras.
         This line of inquiry converges with Genesis evidence. The discovery that Aran survived in stasis transforms the
         Emperor Project from purely historical research into a living-contact problem.
         The awakening of Aran therefore becomes one of the campaign's first moments in which the Imperium encounters a
         person whose existence directly connects modern civilization to an ancient technological civilization.

         16.8 Genesis as a Historical System, Not Merely a Technology
         Genesis is initially encountered as ancient technology, but its significance expands as the Imperium understands its
         relationship to Aran's civilization.
         The discovery of Genesis nodes and subterranean complexes establishes that the ancient civilization did not merely
         leave isolated artifacts. It possessed an interconnected technological and institutional infrastructure.
         This becomes the bridge to the Continuity Project.
         The later understanding of Genesis must therefore preserve both sides of its identity: it is a technological system with
         practical capabilities, and it is also material evidence of an ancient civilization's attempt to preserve continuity across
         catastrophic historical change.

         16.9 The Sanctum Breach and the Birth of the Inquisition


         The early security breach changes the Imperium's understanding of secrecy.
         Until this point, security is primarily about protecting the project from ordinary intrusion. The discovery that something
         had been watching the project since before the Thunder Warrior Project began introduces an entirely different category
         of threat: an observer whose existence predates the Imperium's own extraordinary development.
         Maya's recruitment and the creation of the Inquisition therefore establish counter-intelligence as a permanent
         institutional function rather than a temporary response to one incident.
         The Architect Network becomes a persistent investigation because the initial breach cannot explain the full scope of
         the observation.

         16.10 Aran at the Sanctum
         Once Aran is awakened, the Imperium gains its first living historical witness to the ancient continuity system.
         The Regent's immediate decision to introduce Aran to Warhammer 40,000 is deliberately characteristic of the Regent:
         an extraordinary historical encounter is integrated into an ordinary personal interest. Aran's participation in the tabletop
         game with the Regent and Nadira becomes an early example of cultural translation between ancient and modern
         civilizations.
         The resulting conversations about the Continuity Project become more informative precisely because Aran is treated as
         a person rather than merely as an archaeological specimen.

         16.11 Continuity Core Extraction
         The Continuity Core is initially inseparable from the subterranean Continuity infrastructure. Removing it appears
         impossible because its identity and function are distributed throughout the ancient system.
         Raka and Dimas approach the problem from different sides. Raka addresses the engineering problem of containment
         and isolation; Dimas addresses the biological/systemic problem of preserving function without destructive separation.
         Their combined work produces a method by which the Core can be isolated and integrated into the Sanctum.
         The Core is not installed as a ruler. It becomes an analytical and archival intelligence.
         The Regent subsequently teaches it to play Warhammer 40,000. This interaction establishes the Core's recurring
         relationship with the Regent: it is an ancient intelligence learning the strange cultural habits of the civilization that
         inherited it.

         MORPHOLOGY CLARIFICATION
         This section resolves three previously underspecified pieces of canon: how the
         seven Successors physically reached Earth, how the Imperium can begin


         orbital civilization without a pre-existing dedicated space-industrial civilization,
         and what the seven Successor representatives physically look like.

         I. HOW THE SEVEN SUCCESSORS ARRIVED
         PHYSICALLY ON EARTH
         The seven Successors did not arrive using an Imperial spacecraft, because the
         Imperium did not possess a mature interplanetary transportation industry at the
         time of their arrival.
         Each Successor instead arrived using its own civilizational transportation
         technology. The seven arrivals were coordinated as a diplomatic operation
         after the T74 recognition of the Imperium and the establishment of the
         Convergence Forum.

         The Arrival System — Continuity Transit Corridors
         The seven civilizations retain access to ancient transit infrastructure inherited
         from the pre-Successor/Departed civilizational network. Asterion and Orynd
         were able to reconstruct enough of the relevant transit mathematics to
         establish a temporary, controlled Continuity Transit Corridor terminating in
         cislunar space.
         The corridor is not a permanent Stargate and does not constitute an Imperial
         technology. It is a highly sophisticated Successor-operated transit
         phenomenon using ancient infrastructure, modern Successor stabilization, and
         extremely precise gravitational/navigation control.
         The diplomatic operation worked in four stages:
          1. A Successor vessel or transit-body entered the corridor from its own
             civilization's region of space.
          2. The corridor terminated at a controlled point in high Earth orbit/cislunar
             space rather than directly inside Earth's atmosphere.
          3. The arriving delegation transferred into atmospheric-capable descent craft
             or self-contained physical bodies.
          4. The delegations descended to the designated diplomatic arrival site under
             Imperial observation and security.
         This means the seven Successors are physically present beings, not
         holograms, remote avatars, or purely virtual diplomatic manifestations.


         Why the Imperium could not simply copy this
         The transit system depends on Successor-level knowledge of gravitational
         topology, Continuity infrastructure, navigation, and stabilization. The Imperium
         currently lacks the industrial base, theoretical knowledge, and orbital
         infrastructure necessary to reproduce it independently.
         The existence of the transit corridor therefore does not retroactively give the
         Imperium an established spacefaring transportation industry. It is one of the
         technological ceilings the Imperium has only begun to understand through
         Successor contact.
         The arrival also remains HIGH-AUTHORITY-CLASSIFIED. The general public
         does not know that the seven Successors physically arrived on Earth.

         II. HOW THE IMPERIUM CAN BUILD CATHEDRA
         ORBITALIS WITHOUT A PRE-EXISTING SPACE
         INDUSTRY
         The earlier wording is clarified: the Imperium did not already possess a mature
         orbital civilization, orbital transportation network, or dedicated space-industrial
         base.
         The T74 Imperial–Orynd Orbital Civilization Program is precisely the process by
         which that missing civilizational capability is being created.

         What was missing
         Before the Successor exchange, the Imperium had no mature equivalent of:
              a permanent orbital manufacturing economy;
              a dedicated orbital logistics chain;
              large-scale orbital construction yards;
              routine heavy cargo movement between Earth and orbit;
              permanent civilian orbital habitation;
              an institutionalized space-industrial workforce;
              a mature spacefaring transportation architecture.
         The correct term for the missing civilizational feature is broadly orbital
         industrial base / space-industrial infrastructure, with the larger
         developmental category being spacefaring infrastructure.


         What Orynd actually contributes
         Orynd does not simply hand the Imperium a finished orbital city.
         Orynd provides the initial orbital seed infrastructure: autonomous construction
         systems, orbital fabrication technology, high-efficiency propulsion, structural
         assembly systems, gravitational engineering support, and the technical
         knowledge required to establish an industrial foothold above Earth.
         The Imperium supplies human personnel, terrestrial materials, administrative
         authority, selected technology, security, cultural/architectural direction, and
         progressively greater amounts of locally manufactured hardware.

         The Orbital Seed Foundry
         The first physical stage is a small Orynd-assisted orbital construction complex,
         informally designated the Orbital Seed Foundry.
         Its purpose is not habitation. Its purpose is to manufacture and assemble the
         next generation of orbital infrastructure in orbit.
         This solves the otherwise circular problem of "how do you build a gigantic
         orbital structure without already having an orbital industry?":
         Orynd supplies the initial bootstrap capability → the Seed Foundry
         manufactures orbital components → orbital infrastructure expands →
         Imperial industry progressively takes over more of the production chain → a
         genuine Imperial orbital industrial base emerges.

         Cathedra Orbitalis
         Cathedra Orbitalis is therefore not something the Imperium secretly possessed
         before T74. It is the first major civilizational project produced by the Imperial
         transition into orbital industry.
         Its 50,000-person initial target is a long-term target, not an immediately existing
         population. The city will be constructed incrementally from the Seed Foundry
         and subsequent orbital manufacturing nodes.
         The Gothic-derived architecture is a deliberate Imperial cultural requirement.
         Orynd provides the engineering framework; the Imperium determines the
         civilization's aesthetic and symbolic language.
         This program consequently creates a new Imperial civilizational feature in real
         time:
         Imperial Orbital Industrialization / Spacefaring Infrastructure.


         No pre-T74 Imperial orbital civilization should be assumed unless separately
         established by later canon.

         Closer examination reveals subtle signs of extensive posthuman modification:
         unusually precise eye movement, extremely controlled breathing, faintly altered
         skin reflectivity, and a cardiovascular system capable of functioning under
         conditions far beyond normal human tolerance.
         Sael's body is biological, but it is not baseline human.
         Impression: the one who could walk into a human crowd and initially pass
         unnoticed.

         Physical form: Amphibious and strongly non-human. Approximately 2.3 metres

         Nereth possesses large black eyes protected by a transparent secondary
         membrane, external breathing structures along the sides of the neck and
         ribcage, and fine sensory filaments extending from the forearms and back of
         the head.
         The body can breathe both atmospheric and water-rich environments and
         possesses powerful internal fluid-regulation mechanisms.
         Impression: elegant aquatic humanoid rather than "fish person"; every
         anatomical feature appears adapted for a civilization whose environment is
         inseparable from planetary water systems.

         4. Cael Orun — Orynd
         Physical form: A deliberately constructed high-gravity-adapted synthetic
         humanoid approximately 2.4 metres tall. The frame is exceptionally slender but
         mechanically dense, with long limbs, narrow shoulders, and a dark metallic-
         ceramic exterior resembling matte obsidian with subtle bronze structural lines.
         Cael's face contains no conventional mouth. Communication is primarily
         vocalized through a resonance chamber in the upper torso, supplemented by
         facial expression generated through controlled changes in the head's surface
         geometry.
         The body possesses no visible conventional joints at several locations;
         movement is produced through distributed artificial musculature and localized
         gravitational control.
         Impression: an architectural figure given physical life — austere, monumental,
         and engineered rather than biological.

         5. Elyra Sen — Seraphe
         Physical form: A graceful biological humanoid approximately 1.8 metres tall.
         Elyra appears almost entirely human at first glance, but the body is a
         deliberately optimized biological system.
         The skin is unusually smooth and carries faint shifting pigmentation patterns
         that respond to temperature, emotion, and biochemical state. Hair is fine and


         highly adaptive. The eyes contain a second translucent layer and exhibit
         unusual colour variation under different light.
         Elyra's body heals minor injuries rapidly and can alter certain physiological
         parameters deliberately. The appearance is beautiful without looking artificial:
         Seraphe's philosophy treats biology itself as an expressive and adaptive
         medium.
         Impression: the most recognizably alive of the seven, but also the one whose
         biology most clearly exceeds baseline humanity.

         6. Veyr-9 — Velith
         Physical form: Veyr-9 does not possess a single permanent biological body. Its
         diplomatic manifestation is a cryptographic embodiment: a roughly humanoid
         figure approximately 1.9 metres tall composed of a suspended field of
         thousands of microscopic luminous particles and thin geometric filaments.
         From a distance it resembles a person made from extremely fine silver-black
         dust. Up close, the body continuously reorganizes itself into shifting geometric
         structures. There is no visible skin, blood, skeleton, or conventional organ
         system.
         Veyr-9 can temporarily produce a more solid tactile surface for diplomatic
         interaction, but its true embodiment is distributed information architecture.
         Impression: a person-shaped encryption system that has learned to inhabit
         physical space.

         7. Morrow — Eidolon
         Physical form: Deliberately variable.
         Morrow's default diplomatic embodiment is a androgynous human-like body

         Impression: unmistakably a person, but a person for whom a body is an

         3. Genesis — Correct Developmental History
         Genesis was originally recovered as advanced technology during an assault on
         one of the Architect's active facilities. It was therefore not originally an Imperial
         invention and not originally designed as Jakarta infrastructure.
         After recovery, Genesis was progressively studied and integrated into the
         Sanctum. Its earliest major practical role inside the Imperium was supporting
         industrialization and manufacturing, where its ability to autonomously expand
         production capability made it exceptionally valuable.
         Genesis can autonomously expand and replicate aspects of its industrial
         infrastructure with relatively little routine human interaction. However, it still
         requires human involvement for critical decisions, strategic direction,
         exceptional-risk actions, and decisions that materially alter the system's long-
         term trajectory.
         This distinction is fundamental: Genesis is highly autonomous but is not an
         unbounded sovereign intelligence and does not possess unrestricted authority
         over the Imperium.
         Its later integration into Jakarta therefore represents a major technological
         escalation built upon earlier Sanctum experience. The Imperium did not
         suddenly discover that Genesis could build cities; it accumulated operational
         knowledge from years of using Genesis inside the Sanctum's
         industrial/manufacturing environment and then applied that knowledge to
         metropolitan reconstruction.

         T70's city-wide Genesis integration is the culmination of the earlier Sanctum
         industrialization program. The Continuity Core provides modelling, auditing,
         optimization, and dependency monitoring; Genesis provides autonomous

         Genesis may become deeply integrated into the city's regenerative

         The canonical figure is the Custodian, singular. He is not a group called the
         Custodes.

         epistemic: he possesses knowledge concerning ancient civilization, ancient
         records, the Continuity Project, and related unresolved systems that cannot be
         reconstructed reliably from modern Imperial archives alone.
         The Custodian is advisory, not a constitutional ruler. He does not command the
         Senatorum, override the Regent, or directly govern Imperial institutions. His
         authority derives from knowledge, historical continuity, and the credibility of his
         records.

         what he believes, and what remains unknown. His knowledge does not
