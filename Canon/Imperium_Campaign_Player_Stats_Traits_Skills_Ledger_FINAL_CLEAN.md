         REGENT PANOPLY — CANONICAL
         EQUIPMENT SUMMARY
         Status: Canonical; integrated into the Regent's established equipment
         configuration.
         The Regent Panoply is a compact Terminator-equivalent power armor system
         with zero capability reduction. Its reduced volume is achieved through
         advanced materials, component miniaturization, dense systems integration,
         and distributed redundancy rather than by removing functions. It retains
         Terminator-class protection, powered strength/mobility, full environmental
         sealing and life support, integrated sensors/comms, medical monitoring, and
         redundant power and control systems.
         Iron Halo: Integrated active defensive array providing threat detection, tracking,
         sensor fusion, electronic countermeasures, and active interception support. It


         is a functional defensive system, not merely ornament.
         Purity Seals: Purely decorative ceremonial elements. They have no mechanical
         function and serve as records of oaths, victories, institutions, history, and
         Imperial identity.
         Cape & Trim: Elaborately decorated, durable ceremonial textiles and armor
         ornamentation. Primarchial in visual language and deliberately excessive, but
         non-essential to the armor's core function.
         Design principle: The Panoply combines extreme practical engineering with
         deliberate ceremonial grandeur. Its aesthetic is a campaign-native evolution of
         the Primarchial panoply: a genuine military system that also functions as a
         visible symbol of the Regent, the Imperium, and its history.

         Zero
         The Regent is the campaign's lore-accurate Thunder Warrior without the

         transhuman performance of the Thunder Warrior design while remaining
         biologically stable and fully functional.

         Thunder Warrior concept as established by campaign canon.

         Zero is itself an established physical/biological anomaly. His extraordinary
         capabilities are therefore not constrained to ordinary human performance.
         Feats far beyond human limits are valid when supported by his established
         Thunder Warrior capability. Physics still governs the consequences: force,
         momentum, trajectory, energy transfer, structural effects, environmental
         interaction, and causality must remain coherent. The engine must not nerf
         established Thunder Warrior feats merely because an ordinary human could
         not perform them.

         Canonical Thunder Warriors are associated with severe long-term biological

              degenerative collapse;
              shortened lifespan caused by the Thunder Warrior template;
              progressive insanity caused by the template;
              spontaneous physiological failure caused by the template;

         These are not hidden future defects waiting to activate. They are removed by
         established campaign canon.
         Other injuries, diseases, environmental hazards, toxins, deprivation, or
         genuinely new biological threats may still affect him according to the
         campaign's physical rules. "Flawless Thunder Warrior" means the Thunder
         Warrior design itself does not contain a built-in failure state; it does not mean

         The Regent is the biological ancestor of the campaign's Astartes lineage.

         framework. Astartes comparisons are used only when establishing relative
